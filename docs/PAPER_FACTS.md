# Paper facts

[Source: arXiv:2409.03334v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2409.03334v1), 5 September 2024. Reported prototype results do not certify this 2026 CAD snapshot.

## Design and FEA (II-A; Figs. 1-2)

- Envelope: diameter 220 mm; height 180 mm; approximately 40 kg.
- Gap: mean radius 80 mm; width 15 mm; height 50 mm.
- Magnet rings: radii 20/65 mm; height 25 mm; DT4C yokes.
- Materials: samarium cobalt (Sm2Co17/SmCo) or neodymium iron boron (NdFeB).
- Compensation: height 5 mm; width 0.4 mm; uniformity <10^-3 extends 23→35 mm.
- Split: z=15 mm; SmCo/NdFeB equilibrium openings 22/41 mm.
- Assembly peak: 5815 N including gravity (III-B, Table I).
- Annealed DT4C: relative permeability >2×10^4 at working fields (II-B2, Fig.4).

## Measurements (IV; Figs. 9-12)

- Center: SmCo 0.45 T; NdFeB 0.59 T.
- NdFeB thermal time constant: approximately 2 h; coefficient: −1.1×10^-3 K^-1.
- Bifilar: 1360 turns/coil; 0.2 mm wire; approximately 395 Ω/coil.
- Bl: >400 Tm per bifilar winding; >800 Tm for the conventional single-coil configuration.
- DC-extrapolated inductance: L(z)=1.3725−650.3z² H; z in meters.
- Profile depends on split-interface reluctance and measurement-coil radius.
- Current effect characterized; 10^-8 mass-realization accuracy remains a target.
